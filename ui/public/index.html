WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

x402 moves money.
SPUR defines what the money means.

01

Not a Payment

x402 moves the payment. SPUR defines the economic obligation that exists before payment is consumed. The commitment is the product.

02

Not Escrow

Escrow holds funds. SPUR creates an active economic right whose settlement is conditional on machine-verifiable delivery.

03

Conditional Settlement

No verification = no settlement. Bad work = $0. The invariant is mechanical, not a UI promise. Failed delivery does not become payment.

04

Obligation-Backed Liquidity

The economic right itself becomes financeable. Suppliers get advances against specific bounded obligations, not against generic reputation.
